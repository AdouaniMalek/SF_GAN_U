## Benchmark Datasets

The experiments were conducted on five publicly available healthcare datasets covering different clinical prediction tasks, sample sizes, feature types, and levels of class imbalance. Together, these datasets provide a diverse benchmark for evaluating the utility, fairness, privacy, and statistical realism of synthetic tabular data.

### Cervical Cancer

The **Cervical Cancer** dataset contains demographic, behavioral, and medical-history variables collected for cervical cancer risk assessment. It is the smallest and most imbalanced benchmark, with only 52 positive cases among 858 observations.

- **Source:** [Kaggle - Cervical Cancer](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ranzeet013/cervical-cancer-dataset)

### Cardiovascular Disease

The **Cardiovascular Disease** dataset contains objective measurements, clinical examination results, and self-reported information used to predict the presence of cardiovascular disease. With 70,000 observations and an approximately balanced target distribution, it represents a large-scale binary-classification setting.

- **Source:** [Kaggle — Cardiovascular Disease Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sulianova/cardiovascular-disease-dataset)

### Alzheimer’s Disease

The **Alzheimer’s Disease** dataset includes demographic, lifestyle, medical-history, clinical, and cognitive-assessment variables used to predict an Alzheimer’s disease diagnosis. It contains 2,149 observations and a moderately imbalanced outcome.

- **Source:** [Kaggle — Alzheimer’s Disease Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rabieelkharoua/alzheimers-disease-dataset)

### Diabetes Indicators

Derived from the **2015 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS)**, the **Diabetes Indicators** dataset contains health indicators, lifestyle factors, healthcare-access information, and demographic characteristics used for diabetes-status prediction. It is the largest benchmark, with 253,680 observations.

- **Source:** [Kaggle — Diabetes Health Indicators Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/alexteboul/diabetes-health-indicators-dataset)

### Pediatric Appendicitis

The **Pediatric Appendicitis** dataset contains clinical observations, laboratory measurements, clinical scores, and ultrasonographic findings from pediatric patients evaluated for suspected appendicitis. The diagnosis variable is used as the binary prediction target. The cohort includes 782 patients, of whom 465 were diagnosed with appendicitis.

- **Source:** [UCI Machine Learning Repository — Regensburg Pediatric Appendicitis](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/938/regensburg+pediatric+appendicitis)
